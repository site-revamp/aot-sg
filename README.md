# Vanguard Facility Management — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Vanguard Facility Management Pte. Ltd. provides engineering-led facilities management and specialist maintenance services in Singapore. Its customers include property operators and organisations across commercial, industrial, aviation and other technical sectors.

The proposed homepage prioritises integrated maintenance contracts, engineering support and emergency enquiries, with clearer routes through the company's extensive service offering.

## Main Improvements

1. **Consistent, substantiated performance statistics**  
   Replace conflicting operating-history figures and zero-value placeholders with verified, clearly labelled metrics. Omit unconfirmed statistics to prevent misleading or incomplete credibility claims.

2. **Clearer priority for corporate service enquiries**  
   Give contract enquiries, site assessments and emergency support prominent entry points. Keep product shopping as a secondary pathway so the featured-products section does not compete with the main facilities-management journey.

3. **More structured service and industry navigation**  
   Group the broad service range into understandable pathways, connecting integrated facilities management, technical maintenance and specialist engineering with relevant industries. Help visitors identify suitable expertise and reach the appropriate enquiry action.

## Design Improvements

- Establish a clearer hierarchy between the main proposition, services, project evidence and contact actions.
- Use consistent headings, spacing and service layouts to make technical information easier to scan.
- Give relevant project examples and confirmed credentials a focused place in the page structure.
- Keep emergency assistance visually distinct from routine contract and product enquiries.

## Implementation Status

This summary documents the proposed redesign direction. Completed implementation has not been verified against a redesigned homepage draft.
