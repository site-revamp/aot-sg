Common questions

Some useful details to help you identify the right service route.

What types of services does AOT provide?

AOT's published service information covers plumbing, water heaters, air-conditioning, pumps, water tanks and related mechanical and electrical service areas.

Does AOT handle residential and commercial enquiries?

The company presents a range of services relevant to residential customers and property or facilities requirements. Contact AOT to confirm the scope available for your particular site.

How can I contact AOT?

You can call +65 6816 2462, send a WhatsApp message to +65 8151 8857, or use the official contact page.
